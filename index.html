Your story deserves to be met with kindness

You may be carrying worries that have stayed with you for a long time. You may be feeling anxious, overwhelmed or simply not quite yourself. Perhaps relationships feel difficult, old memories have resurfaced, or life has begun to lose its sense of direction.

I believe each of us carries an inner world that deserves to be met with kindness, curiosity and compassion. Our struggles are rarely random. They often have roots in our relationships, our childhood experiences and the ways we have learned to navigate life.

Together, we can gently begin to understand your story and discover what it may be asking of you.

How therapy begins, step by step

Finding the right therapist is an important part of the process, and there is never any pressure to continue.

1
Step 1

A free 30-minute conversation

Either by telephone or online. You can tell me what has brought you to therapy, ask any questions you may have, and discover whether you feel comfortable talking with me.

2
Step 2

Your first session

Fifty minutes, in person in Leicester or online. A warm, thoughtful and confidential space where we begin to explore your experience together.

3
Step 3

We review as we go

There is no set timescale. Some people benefit from a brief period of therapy, others explore their inner world over longer. We review our work together as it unfolds.

Qualifications and experience matter, but feeling genuinely heard, understood and accepted is often where meaningful change begins.

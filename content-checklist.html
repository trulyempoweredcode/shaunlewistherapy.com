Content checklist

A short list of things that would make the new site even stronger.

Your new site is built entirely from the words already on your existing website, plus the photographs of your room that you sent over. Nothing has been invented or added that you hadn't already said.

These are the gaps we spotted while building it. None of them stop the site going live, but each one would make it work harder for you.

Worth doing first

Client feedbackYour site currently has no testimonials at all, and we haven't written any. For a therapist this is usually the single biggest thing a visitor looks for. Even two or three short, anonymised lines ("S., Leicester") would make a real difference on the homepage and the contact page. Only send us feedback you have permission to publish.
The original file of your photographThe only picture of you we could find was 300 pixels wide, which is very small for a modern site and for your Google search listing. If you can find the original from your photographer or phone, please send it. We have used the small one carefully in the meantime.
Larger photographs of your roomThe room photos you sent are 640 pixels wide. They look good at the size we've used them, but bigger originals would let us use them far more boldly. If you still have them on your phone, the full-size versions would be ideal.

Please confirm

Your working hoursYour email auto-reply says you respond within 24 hours, Monday to Thursday, and that Friday and weekend messages are answered the next working day. That's useful for visitors to know, but we haven't put it on the site because it came from an auto-reply rather than from you. Would you like it added?
Your practice addressThe site currently describes two areas, "Knighton Fields Road East, close to the University of Leicester" and "Leicester, close to the city centre", exactly as your old site did. If you're happy to publish a full address and postcode for the main practice, it would help you appear in local Google searches. If you'd rather not, that's absolutely fine.
A cancellation policyYour old site didn't have one, so we haven't written one. Most therapists publish their notice period. If you have one, send us the wording and we'll add it to the fees page.
The design form said "Marketing"The onboarding form recorded your primary service as "Marketing", which we're confident was a slip in the dropdown. The site has been built as counselling and psychotherapy throughout.
A logoYour current site's "logo" is a crop of the same stock water-droplet picture used as the banner, so we haven't carried it across. Your name is set in the site's own typeface instead, which looks deliberate and clean. If you'd like a proper logo designed, just say.

Worth considering later

Competitor psychotherapy practices in Leicester commonly carry a few things your site doesn't yet. None are essential, and we haven't added anything you didn't say.

An "areas I work with" pageA page listing the difficulties people commonly bring (anxiety, depression, bereavement, trauma and so on) tends to bring in a lot of search traffic. We'd need you to tell us which of these you actually work with, so we don't claim anything on your behalf.
Your BACP register listingA link to your entry on the BACP register is a strong trust signal. Send us the URL and we'll add it.
Your qualifications in fullThe site says you are Senior Accredited with the BACP, aligned with SCoPEd Column C, and that you trained in couples therapy with an experienced Relate psychotherapist. If you'd like your specific diplomas and training listed on the About page, send the details.
A writing sectionGiven your interest in dreams, Jungian work and the unconscious, occasional short articles would suit you well and help people find you. We can switch a blog on whenever you want one.

About the pictures on the site

The colours of the whole site were taken from your own photographs: the honey pine floor, the cream walls, the dusty rose in the rug and the warm lamplight.

Four of your room photographs appear on the homepage. The wider room scenes used in the main banner, the couples page and the closing panels were created for you to match your room, because the originals weren't large enough to fill a full-width banner. The stock water-droplet picture from your old site has not been reused.
